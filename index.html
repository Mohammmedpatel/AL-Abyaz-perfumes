Rooted in Mumbai, Inspired by Arabia

Al-Abyaz Perfumes & Attars began with a simple belief: fragrance should be honest — real ingredients, fair prices, and a person on the other end of every order.

Every attar and perfume in our collection is chosen (and many blended) with that in mind, from our base in Jogeshwari, Mumbai, to doorsteps across India.
