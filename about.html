How We Started

From Jogeshwari to Every Doorstep

Al-Abyaz Perfumes & Attars started as a small, personal venture in Jogeshwari, Mumbai — built on a simple idea: fragrance should feel personal, and buying it shouldn't feel transactional.

What began as sharing a handful of attars with friends and family has grown into a full collection of perfumes, attars and oud, still packed and sent out by hand, with every order confirmed over a real WhatsApp conversation — not a checkout page.
